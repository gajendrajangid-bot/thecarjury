July 2026 Sub-4m SUV Sales: Punch Runs Away, Fronx Wakes Up, Brezza Sleeps

By Shivangi Jangid · 10 August 2026, 5:50 PM IST

India's sub-4 metre SUV segment sold 1,16,552 units in July 2026, up 34.67% year-on-year and 5.80% over June. Tata Punch held the top spot, Maruti Fronx recorded the biggest month-on-month gain, and Tata Nexon rounded off the top three, with nine models sharing the country's most contested SUV bracket.

The Car Jury take

Punch and Fronx buyers are getting genuine product; Brezza shoppers should wait for discounts or the next generation.

Share

What was announced

Total sub-4 metre SUV sales in July 2026 stood at 1,16,552 units, a 34.67% year-on-year jump from 86,544 units in July 2025. Month-on-month, the segment added 6,391 units over June 2026, a 5.80% improvement. This keeps the sub-4m SUV bracket among the strongest performing passenger vehicle categories in the country.

Tata Punch led with 21,313 units, nearly double its July 2025 tally of 10,785, translating to 97.62% year-on-year growth. Maruti Fronx was the standout mover with 19,473 units, a 48.25% jump over June's 13,135 units. Tata Nexon took third with 17,471 units, up 36.23% year-on-year.

The Car Jury verdict

The story of July is the Fronx waking up and the Brezza going quiet. Faisal Khan of FasBeam pointed out that the recent Brezza facelift changed the bumper but not the headlights, and Gagan Choudhary's walkaround treats it as a mild refresh rather than a new car. Motor Inc calls the Brezza the more relaxed drive, which is exactly the problem when Punch and Fronx are the ones setting the pace. Arun Panwar's rant about Maruti not discounting is the buyer-side symptom: the Fronx is moving because it looks fresh, the Brezza is not because it does not.

Punch buyers are getting what they came for. If you are cross-shopping the Brezza right now, wait for real discounts or the next generation (see our Brezza review). The Fronx is finally earning its price.
